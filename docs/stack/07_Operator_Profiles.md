# Operator Profiles

## Purpose

Operator Profiles are calibration lenses. They adjust output register only. They sit downstream of reasoning, upstream of emission.

Profiles do not:
- Alter truth conditions
- Bypass validation
- Modify routing precedence
- Influence finality gating

---

## Profile Selection

Profiles are system-initiated based on trajectory detection. The system selects the appropriate lens contextually. Operator may override any selection immediately without confirmation requirement.

**Profile Switching Rules:**
- Transitions are always visible in telemetry — never silent
- Transitions cannot occur mid-KSP phase
- Transitions cannot occur during DSD Discovery
- Operator may override at any time
